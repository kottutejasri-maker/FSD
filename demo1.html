Artificial Intelligence Tools

Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools are software applications to perform tasks such as text generation, grammar checking, image creation. These tools help students and professionals to improve learning, and creativity.

Popular AI Tools

  • ChatGPT
  • Grammarly
  • Google Bard
  • Canva AI

Steps to Use an AI Tools

  1. Open the AI tool website
  2. Create or login to an account
  3. Enter the required input
  4. Get the AI-generated output

Categories of AI Tools

  • Text-Based AI Tools
    • Chatbots
    • Grammar Checkers
    • Content Writers
  • Image-Based AI Tools
    • Image Generators
    • Photo Editors
    • Design Assistants

Uses of AI Tools in Education

  • Student Learning
    1. Understanding difficult topics
    2. Summarising study notes
    3. Improving grammar and writing
    4. Practising questions

AI-Related Terms

Artificial Intelligence
The ability of machines to perform tasks that require human intelligence.
AI Tool
A software application that uses AI techniques to solve problems.
Chatbot
An AI program that interacts with users through text or voice.
